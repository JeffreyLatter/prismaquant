\caption{\textbf{The rate--distortion frontier is convex, and a sharp knee is a
property of the format \emph{menu}, not of the sensitivity distribution.}
\textbf{(a)} On Qwen3-4B (RTN weight-only, measured KL-vs-BF16), the
two-rung $\{$NVFP4,\,BF16$\}$ menu yields a near-linear frontier; adding the
intermediate-efficiency FP8 rung introduces a cost-per-bit discontinuity that
bends it into a sharp knee near $8$~bpp ($6\!\times$ lower KL at matched bytes).
\textbf{(b)} The shipped Qwen3.6-27B served-vLLM frontier (two-rung)
is genuinely convex on linear axes (chord curvature $24.5\%$; the per-Linear
cost-per-bit has weighted Gini $0.45$, far from democratic); it only \emph{looks}
linear when plotted as $\log_{10}\kl$ over the narrow $4.5$--$6.0$~bpp window
where production allocations live. A better cost (AURA) shifts this frontier
\emph{down}, orthogonally to its curvature.}
\label{fig:aura_rd_geometry}
\end{figure}
